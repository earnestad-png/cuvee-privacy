Delete your account

You can request deletion of your Cuvée account and associated data (learning progress, wishlist, tasting journal entries, and optional photos).

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the same address you use to sign in to Cuvée.
  2. Use the subject line: Account deletion request.
  3. Include your account email in the message body.

Email support.cuvee@gmail.com

We will delete or anonymize your account data within a reasonable period, unless we must retain something for legal reasons. You will receive a confirmation reply when the request is completed.
