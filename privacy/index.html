Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 August 2026

App: Cuvée (com.cuvee.wineapp)

Operator: Cuvée

Contact: support.cuvee@gmail.com

This policy describes how Cuvée (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the mobile app and related services.

1. What Cuvée is

Cuvée is a wine education app: lessons, daily learning games, tasting journal, and optional wine discovery. You may create an account so progress and journal data sync across devices.

2. Information we collect

Account and authentication

  • Email address
  • Authentication identifiers from our auth provider (Supabase)
  • If you choose Sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information Google shares for sign-in (typically email and name), subject to Google’s settings

You can also sign in with email + password or a magic link sent to any email address (Gmail is not required).

App data you create

  • Learning progress (completed lessons, points, streaks)
  • Wishlist and “tried” wines
  • Tasting journal entries (notes, structure ratings, aromas, optional photos)
  • Preferences such as shopping market

Technical data

  • Device and app diagnostics as provided by the platform / crash tooling you enable
  • Deep-link and session tokens needed to keep you signed in

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account
  • Sync your progress and journal across devices
  • Provide app features you request (games, lessons, discovery)
  • Send account emails (confirmation, magic link, password reset)
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs
  • Comply with law when required

4. Processors / service providers

Provider Role
Supabase Authentication, database, and file storage (e.g. tasting photos)
Google Optional Google sign-in (OAuth)
Expo / EAS App build and update delivery infrastructure

These providers process data on our behalf under their terms and security practices.

5. Photos

Tasting photos are optional. If you attach a photo, it is stored in our cloud storage associated with your account so it can sync. You can remove journal entries (and related media) from the app; contact us if you need help deleting residual files.

6. Retention

We keep account and sync data while your account is active. If you request deletion, we delete or anonymize personal data within a reasonable period unless we must retain something for legal reasons.

7. Your choices

  • Use the app with email/password or magic link instead of Google
  • Skip optional photos
  • Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing support.cuvee@gmail.com
  • Sign out in Profile to end the local session

8. Children

Cuvée is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Alcohol content

The app teaches about wine and may show wine product information. It does not sell alcohol directly. Please use the app responsibly and follow local laws.

10. International processing

Data may be processed in the EU, US, or other regions where our providers operate. We rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will revise the effective date above and, when changes are material, provide additional notice in the app or by email when appropriate.

12. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support.cuvee@gmail.com
